How long does delivery take?

From the U.S. warehouse, Economy (USPS) takes 5–8 business days and Priority Express 2–4, counted from the order date. Items that ship from Brazil — marked on the product page with a Brazilian flag — take 11–16 business days.

Is shipping free?

Economy shipping is free on orders over $50 and $8.90 under that. Priority Express is $9.90.

Can I return an order?

You have 30 days from delivery to request one. The item has to come back unused, in its original packaging, with the receipt — and the return has to be requested first, at info.usa@ybera.com. Packages sent back without a request are not accepted.

Is the keratin formaldehyde-free?

Yes. The formulas are formaldehyde-free and safe on color-treated hair.
