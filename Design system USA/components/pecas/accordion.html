How to use

Wash with a clarifying shampoo and towel-dry. Apply from mid-length to ends, avoiding the scalp. Leave for 20 minutes, then blow-dry and flat-iron in thin sections.

Ingredients

Aqua, Cetearyl Alcohol, Hydrolyzed Keratin, Behentrimonium Chloride, Glycerin, Argania Spinosa Kernel Oil, Panthenol.
